Privacy Policy

Walks with Yaya ("we", "us") respects your privacy. We only collect what we need to arrange and provide great care for your dog.

What we collect

  • Details you send us — your name, your dog's name, email, phone number, and anything you tell us about your pup.
  • Booking details — services requested, dates, home-access instructions, and vet/emergency contacts.
  • Basic, anonymous website analytics (if enabled) such as pages viewed — never used to identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule walks or visits.
  • To provide care safely — including contacting your vet in an emergency.
  • To send you report cards, updates, and (only if you opt in) occasional news.

How we protect & share it

We do not sell your information. We share it only where necessary to provide the service (for example, an emergency vet) or where required by law. Keys and access codes are stored securely and are never labelled with your address. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing us.

Cookies

This website itself sets no tracking cookies. If privacy-friendly analytics are added in future, this section will be updated to say what they measure and how to opt out.

Booking & Cancellation

  • Meet & greet: the first hello is free and no-obligation.
  • Cancellations: cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 12 hours before a booking. Inside 12 hours, a 50% fee applies so we can hold your slot.
  • Payment: invoiced weekly / due on the day of service. Accepted methods will be confirmed at your meet & greet.
  • Holidays: bookings on public holidays may carry a surcharge, agreed in advance.

Care & Safety

  • Dogs must be up to date on core vaccinations and flea/tick prevention before their first group walk.
  • We're insured and bonded, and certified in canine first-aid & CPR.
  • In an emergency we will always act in your dog's best interest and contact you and your vet as soon as possible. You authorise necessary emergency care up to a limit agreed at your meet & greet.
  • We reserve the right to decline or end a walk if a dog's behaviour poses a risk to itself, other dogs, or people.
