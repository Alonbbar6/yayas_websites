Good questions

Frequently asked

Is Yaya insured and background-checked?+

Absolutely. Walks with Yaya is fully insured and bonded, and Yaya is background-checked and certified in canine first-aid & CPR. Your pup and your home are in safe hands.

Do you require vaccinations?+

Yes — for the safety of all our dogs we ask that pups are up to date on core vaccinations and flea/tick prevention before their first group walk.

How do you access my home?+

Whatever you're comfortable with: a lockbox, a spare key, or a smart lock code. Keys are stored securely and never labelled with your address.

What's your cancellation policy?+

Life happens! Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 12 hours before a walk. Inside that window a 50% fee applies so I can hold your slot.

My dog is reactive or nervous — can you help?+

Definitely. Reactive and shy dogs get solo walks and a slower, trust-first approach. We'll talk through triggers at the meet & greet and go at your pup's pace.

What happens in bad weather?+

We walk rain or shine (with towel-offs on the house). In genuinely unsafe conditions — storms, extreme heat — we switch to indoor play and potty breaks instead.
